Roast Pork and Bondage: Paintings by Mack Brim at Blah Blah Gallery

A review published with Philly Art Blog on February 21, 2025.

Artblog’s new contributor, Joni Sullivan, writes about the exhibit, “Playing House,” a charged feminist look at how cherished childhood dreams don’t match grown up reality. Blah Blah Gallery hosts the exhibit, a gallery that supports women and non-binary artists, now located in the midst of a bustling non-arts corridor — the Italian Market area. Joni talks about the neighborhood and the art, and comments, “The lovely real life grit of South 9th Street accepts a feminist gallery as readily as it accepts a raunchy sandwich shop. That is a very beautiful, very Philly, very 2025 thing.”

Installation view of Playing House, Blah Blah Gallery, Philadelphia, PA, 2025. Image courtesy of the gallery.
